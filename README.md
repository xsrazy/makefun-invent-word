## The word

> **Wavestray (n.)** A wave returned to a stranger who was waving at someone behind you.

Everyone has lived this moment: you wave back at someone, smile and all,
and a half-second later realise they were waving at the person standing
right behind you. English had no single word for it — until now.
*Wavestray* is "wave" + "stray": a wave that went astray, to the wrong
person. Sayable, spellable, memorable, and built from plain English
morphemes the way "castaway" and "runaway" are.
